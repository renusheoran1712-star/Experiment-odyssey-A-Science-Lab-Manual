🎧 Listen & learn

Green plants make food by photosynthesis. Tiny pores called stomata allow gases to move into and out of a leaf. In Moll’s half-leaf experiment, potassium hydroxide absorbs carbon dioxide inside a bottle.

⚠️ Safety first

This is a simulation, not a replacement for the practical. KOH is a strong alkali and must be prepared by the teacher. Wear goggles and gloves. Never heat alcohol over a flame; use a hot-water bath under teacher supervision. Do not handle chemicals without your teacher.
